Privacy Policy

Scramble & Gamble  |  Last updated: April 2, 2026

Scramble & Gamble ("the App") is a golf trip scoring and wagering app designed for use among friend groups. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information. We take your privacy seriously and have designed the App to collect only what is necessary to make it work.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use the App:

  • Account Information: Your email address and display name, collected via Firebase Authentication when you create an account or sign in.
  • Game Data: Scores, prize amounts, bingo progress, challenge results, and other gameplay data you enter during use. This data is stored in Google Firestore and is shared only with the other members of your golf group.
  • Scorecard Images: If you use the scorecard scanning feature, photos you take are sent to the Anthropic Claude API for optical character recognition (OCR). These images are processed transiently and are not permanently stored by us or by Anthropic.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to operate the App:

  • To authenticate your identity and maintain your account
  • To store and sync game data in real time across your golf group's devices
  • To process scorecard images via AI-powered OCR
  • To generate in-game AI commentary (Broadcast Booth) using your game data

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the App.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Location data
  • Contacts or address book
  • Microphone audio
  • Device identifiers beyond what Firebase uses by default
  • Any analytics beyond basic Firebase usage data

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase / Google Firestore (database and authentication) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Anthropic Claude API (scorecard OCR and AI commentary) — anthropic.com/privacy
  • Expo / EAS (app build and update delivery) — expo.dev/privacy

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for commercial purposes. Game data is shared only within your golf group as required to operate the App. The third-party services listed above receive data only as necessary to provide their respective functions.

6. Data Retention

Your account and game data are retained in Firestore for as long as your account exists. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us (see below). Scorecard images are processed in real time and are not retained after the OCR process completes.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 17. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 17. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Security

We rely on Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules to protect your data. Access to game data is restricted to authenticated members of the golf group associated with each trip. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
