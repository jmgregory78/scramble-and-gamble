Support

Scramble & Gamble  |  Last updated: 17 October 2026

Need help with Scramble & Gamble? Email us and we'll get back to you, usually within one business day.

Email: privacy@scrambleandgamble.com

You can also send feedback from inside the app: tap your profile picture, then Send Feedback.

Report a concern

To report objectionable content or a player's behaviour, open the app and go to your profile picture → Account → Legal → Report a concern, or email us at the address above. We review reports within 24 hours.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Profile → Account → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. See the Privacy Policy for what happens to shared event data.

Purchases and refunds

All purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. To manage or cancel a subscription, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Money

Scramble & Gamble records scores and the amounts your group agrees on. It never handles, holds or transfers money. Any settling up happens between players, outside the app.
